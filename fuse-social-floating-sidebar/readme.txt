== Description ==

**Fuse Social Icons** (formerly Fuse Social Floating Sidebar) is the easiest way to connect your visitors to your social profiles and let them share your content — a floating icon bar, share buttons, a widget, and a Gutenberg block, all from one clean settings screen.

Version 6.0 is a complete, from-the-ground-up rebuild: a modern drag-and-drop admin, real social share buttons (new!), a Gutenberg block, and a one-click migrator that carries over every setting from your current install automatically — nothing to reconfigure, nothing lost.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`, or install it directly from your WordPress admin under Plugins → Add New.
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu.
3. Go to the new **Fuse Social** menu to configure your icons and share buttons. If you're upgrading from an older version, you'll be prompted to review your imported settings first.
4. Use the shortcode, widget, or Gutenberg block to place share buttons anywhere, or leave the floating bar to work automatically.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Will I lose my settings when I update to version 6.0? =

No. The first time 6.0 runs, it automatically detects your existing profile links, icon style, and colors from the earlier version and imports them for you. You'll see a one-time review screen confirming exactly what was imported before you save anything — your site keeps working throughout.

= What's actually new in version 6.0? =

A full rebuild: a modern drag-and-drop settings screen, real social share buttons (previously not available), a Gutenberg block, adjustable icon spacing and independent share-button icon sizing, and the automatic settings migrator described above.

= Is Threads support still free? =

Yes. Threads, along with Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit, GitHub, Behance, Flickr, Tumblr, VK, SoundCloud, Stack Overflow, RSS, and Email, are all free — no license required.

= How do I add share buttons to a post or page? =

Turn on Share Buttons in the "Share buttons" tab and they'll auto-insert before your content — or use the `[fuse_share]` shortcode, the Fuse Social widget, or the Gutenberg block to place them exactly where you want.

= What's the difference between free and Pro? =

Free gives you the full floating icon bar and share buttons with the essentials: shapes, sizing, colors, and placement. Pro adds custom icon uploads, per-network colors, more shapes and hover effects, an action-button/chat-bubble mode, richer share placements and live share counts, display rules, and click analytics. See the feature list above.

= Does this work with page builders and themes? =

Yes — the floating bar, share buttons, widget, and Gutenberg block all work independently of your theme or page builder, and the share-button shortcode can be dropped into any builder's text/HTML element.

= Where can I get help? =

Post in the plugin's support forum on WordPress.org, or reach us directly at [fusefloat.com](https://www.fusefloat.com/) — Pro customers get priority support.
